# Brag Plan: Boston 311 Analytics

## Inspection rubric

1. **What is the app?** A production-style analytics system that turns Boston 311 service-request data into citywide KPIs, ZIP-level benchmarks, risk signals, and recurring-issue intelligence.
2. **Most impressive claim:** The app preserves a query-driven PostgreSQL architecture from raw cleaning through analytical views, Power BI semantic modeling, and a live Streamlit product.
3. **Visual hook:** The citywide `84.1%` SLA number contrasted with ZIP 02108's `-3.1%` latest-month gap and "Underperforming vs City" status.
4. **Actual UI to show:** The city overview KPI row, the ZIP selector and benchmark view, the underperforming status panel, and the top-issues result.
5. **Shortest satisfying video:** 21.84 seconds. It is long enough to establish the city baseline, demonstrate the ZIP interaction, show the result, and land the engineering proof.
6. **Tone:** `polished`, interpreted as a restrained civic-data product film with decisive camera moves and no generic startup hype.
7. **Audio:** A steady, clean business bed with three quiet, motion-matched cues: selection, result reveal, and final lockup.
8. **Share caption:** Built Boston 311 Analytics: a PostgreSQL-backed Streamlit product that turns citywide service data into ZIP-level SLA, backlog, and recurring-issue intelligence.
9. **User flow:** Open the city overview -> choose ZIP 02108 -> compare it with Boston -> identify underperformance and recurring neighborhood issues.

## What is this app?

Boston 311 Analytics is a query-driven data product for understanding city service performance across Boston and within individual ZIP codes.

## The angle

City averages are useful, but neighborhood context is where action begins. The film starts with the reassuring citywide SLA number, then uses the product to reveal what that average hides for ZIP 02108. The close proves that the insight is backed by a real Python, PostgreSQL, Power BI, and Streamlit system.
